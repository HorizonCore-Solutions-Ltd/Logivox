└─ Blended CAC: $900 (improving efficiency)

### Lifetime Value (LTV)

**LTV Calculation:**
```
Year 1:
├─ Average ARR: $4,000
├─ Gross Margin: 85%
├─ Customer Lifetime: 5 years
├─ LTV = $4,000 × 85% × 5 = $17,000
└─ LTV:CAC = 14.2:1 ✅

Year 5:
├─ Average ARR: $8,000
├─ Gross Margin: 90%
├─ Customer Lifetime: 6 years (better retention)
├─ LTV = $8,000 × 90% × 6 = $43,200
└─ LTV:CAC = 48:1 ✅✅
```

### Payback Period

```
Year 1: CAC $1,200 / ($4,000 × 85% / 12) = 4.2 months ✅
Year 5: CAC $900 / ($8,000 × 90% / 12) = 1.5 months ✅✅

Target: <12 months (We're at 4.2 months - excellent!)
```

## 📞 Questions for Investors

**Common Questions & Answers:**

**Q: Why such high growth rates?**
A: We're targeting a $12.8B market with a unique product (inventory + security training). No direct competition. Strong unit economics (8.5:1 LTV:CAC) enable aggressive customer acquisition.

**Q: How defensible is this business?**
A: 1) Compliance certifications take 12-18 months to obtain. 2) Deep ERP integrations take months to build. 3) Training content improves with more users (data moat). 4) High switching costs (data migration).

**Q: What if KnowBe4 adds inventory management?**
A: Unlikely - they're focused on security training across all verticals. We're specialized in inventory management. Our training is customized to inventory workflows (different customer base).

**Q: Can you really achieve 1.8% monthly churn?**
A: Yes. 1) Security training = engagement = retention. 2) Compliance requirement = sticky (can't switch mid-audit). 3) Data migration pain. 4) Similar SaaS companies (HubSpot, Salesforce) have 1-2% churn.

**Q: Why so capital efficient?**
A: 1) Modern tech stack (serverless, low infrastructure costs). 2) Low CAC (organic + partnerships). 3) High gross margins (85-90%). 4) Self-service onboarding (low CS costs).
