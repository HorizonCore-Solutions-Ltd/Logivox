# LogiVox Launches New "Cognitive Supply Chain" Module

**Dateline**: March 4, 2026  
**Category**: Product Announcement

---

**LogiVox**, the leader in voice-first warehouse management, today announced the release of its most advanced module yet: the **Cognitive Orchestration & Autonomous Decision Engine**.

This new release marks a paradigm shift for the platform, moving beyond traditional execution into the realm of _autonomous logistics_.

## From "Digital Twin" to "Digital Brain"

While many competitors offer a "Digital Twin" to visualize operations, LogiVox's new engine actively controls them. By integrating a Monte Carlo simulation service directly into the decision loop, the system can predict the outcome of operational choices before they are made.

> "It's like having your best Operations Manager watching every single order, 24/7," said the lead architect. "But this manager can simulate 1,000 scenarios in a millisecond."

## Key Features

1.  **Self-Balancing Network**: The system detects congestion at specific Fulfillment Centers and automatically re-routes orders to balance the load across the global network.
2.  **Profit Protection**: A built-in "Financial Governor" calculates the real-time P&L of every order, blocking transactions that don't meet margin thresholds.
3.  **Zero-Touch Fulfillment**: New "Pass-Through" logic allows inventory to flow through cross-docking hubs without ever being put away, significantly reducing handling costs.

## Enterprise Ready

The module includes enterprise-grade security and traceability, tracking every automated decision in an immutable audit log. It allows large-scale logistics networks to operate with the agility of a startup but the controls of a Fortune 500.

The **Cognitive Orchestration Module** is available immediately for all Enterprise tier customers.
