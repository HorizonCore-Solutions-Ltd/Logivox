# LogiVox Vision & Mission

## Mission Statement

To provide the most adaptable and efficient warehouse management system for modern supply chains—enabling businesses to optimise operations, reduce errors, and scale effortlessly with intelligent automation and real-time visibility.

## Vision (3-Year Horizon)

**By February 2029**, LogiVox will be the trusted WMS platform for 500+ mid-market and enterprise customers across the UK, EU, and North America, processing 2+ billion transactions annually, generating £8–12M ARR, and setting the industry standard for voice-first, multi-tenant warehouse automation.

### North Star Metrics

- **Customer Retention**: 95%+ net retention rate (expansion through additional locations and modules)
- **Customer Success**: 99.9% system uptime, <30 min P1 response time, <2h resolution for critical issues
- **Operational Excellence**: 98%+ feature adoption among users; <5% user support escalations requiring engineering
- **Market Leadership**: Featured in Gartner WMS Magic Quadrant; recognised by industry analysts for innovation in voice and AI

## Core Values

1. **Honest Simplicity**: No false promises. Clear pricing. Transparent technical capabilities. Realistic timelines.
2. **Customer Obsession**: Built for warehouse teams, not spreadsheets. Every feature tested with real operators.
3. **Scalability Without Compromise**: Handle 1,000 SKUs or 1 million. Multi-tenant architecture without performance creep.
4. **Trust Through Transparency**: Full audit trails. GDPR-ready. No hidden data processing. Regular security reports.
5. **Continuous Innovation**: Weekly releases. Customer feedback loops. Proactive roadmap updates.
