# The Problem & LogiVox Solution

## The Modern Warehouse Challenge

### Current State (2026)

Warehouse operations teams are struggling with three interconnected failures:

1. **Systems Fragmentation**
   - Inventory in one system, picking in another, shipping in a third
   - Manual data entry between systems; no real-time sync
   - Data contradictions cause lost inventory, missed shipments, costly recalls
   - Integration projects take 6–12 months

2. **Staffing & Training Crisis**
   - New operators take 3–6 weeks to reach 80% productivity
   - High turnover (35%+ annually in UK logistics); retraining cost per operator ~£2,000
   - Paper-based picking is error-prone (2–5% miss rate); corrections cost 2–3x the original pick cost
   - Aging workforce; Gen Z expects mobile, modern tools

3. **Operational Inefficiency at Scale**
   - Multiple locations = multiple rule sets (no consistency narrative)
   - No real-time visibility → firefighting rather than optimising
   - Peak seasons require temp staff + chaos; off-season = underutilised capacity
   - Hard to spot bottlenecks; easy to make costly mistakes

**The Cost**:

- Average £15K in stockout losses per UK warehouse per year
- 8% of SKUs are missing at any given time
- Picking errors add 3–5 days to fulfillment cycles
- Compliance audits fail 40% of the time; remediation = £25K+ per occurrence

## Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

### Legacy WMS (SAP, Oracle, Kinaxis)

- Enterprise-grade but bloated; 12–18 month implementation
- Expensive: £250K–£500K upfront + £100K+ annual licensing
- Rigid workflows; customisation is a nightmare
- Poor UX; user adoption stalls at 40–50%
- No voice, no mobile-first, no modern AI

### Best-of-Breed Specialist Tools (Wave Management, Quality Systems)

- Feature-rich for one domain; integration headaches with others
- Point solutions create more fragmentation, not less
- High switching costs; vendor lock-in

### Homegrown/Spreadsheet Systems

- Low cost upfront but unsustainable
- No audit trail; compliance nightmare
- Breaks when volume scales; can't hire away domain knowledge

### Modern SaaS WMS (3PL Central, etc.)

- Cloud-native, good UX
- Often optimised for 3PL use case; less flexible for enterprise/retail
- Limited voice integration; clunky mobile
- Limited multi-tenant tenant isolation; data isolation concerns
