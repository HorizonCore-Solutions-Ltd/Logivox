# LogiVox Customer FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

This document serves as the foundation for the public-facing FAQ section on the website and the first line of defense for customer support.

## General Questions

### What is LogiVox?

LogiVox is an enterprise-grade, AI-driven Warehouse Management System (WMS) built for high-volume 3PLs, distribution centers, and modern supply chains. It unifies inventory, labor, yard management, robotics, and logistics into a single, highly extensible platform.

### Who is LogiVox for?

Our platform is designed for mid-market to enterprise operations, including 3PL providers, e-commerce fulfillment centers, wholesale distributors, and manufacturing warehouses managing complex operations, multiple clients, or automation hardware.

### How is LogiVox different from legacy WMS providers (like Oracle or Manhattan)?

Unlike legacy systems that require years to implement and rely on rigid, outdated architectures, LogiVox is built on a modern, cloud-native stack. We offer unparalleled API extensibility, built-in AI for operational orchestration, seamless IoT/Robotics integration, and a highly intuitive user interface that reduces training time from weeks to days.

## Implementation & Integration

### How long does it take to deploy LogiVox?

Implementation timelines vary based on operational complexity. However, due to our modern architecture and robust data migration tools, standard single-site deployments can go live in 60-90 days, compared to the 9-12 months typically required by legacy vendors.

### Does LogiVox integrate with my existing ERP or Shopify store?

Yes. LogiVox has a robust API surface (over 400 endpoints) and pre-built connectors for major ERPs (SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics), CRM platforms, and e-commerce storefronts (Shopify, Magento, WooCommerce).

### Can it connect to our automated hardware (conveyors, sorters, robotics)?

Absolutely. LogiVox includes a dedicated IoT & Sensor Network module and Robotics Automation Engine, allowing seamless two-way communication with automated storage and retrieval systems (AS/RS), AGVs, AMRs, and standard material handling equipment.

## Operations & Features

### Does LogiVox support 3PL (multi-client) operations?

Yes. Our 3PL Multi-Client module natively supports distinct billing profiles, segregated inventory, client-specific workflows, and customized customer portals within a single facility.

### How does the pricing work?

LogiVox employs a tiered SaaS pricing model based on facility count, user volume, and specific module requirements, plus a one-time implementation and training fee. Please contact our sales team for a custom quote.

## Security & Support

### Is our data secure?

Security is our top priority. LogiVox is SOC 2 compliant, utilizing end-to-end encryption (TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest), Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), and rigorous automated security testing.

### What kind of support do you offer?

We offer comprehensive support tiers, including 24/7 critical incident response, designated Technical Account Managers for Enterprise tiers, and an extensive online Knowledge Base.
