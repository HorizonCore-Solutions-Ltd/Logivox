## Presentation Tips

**Delivery Guidelines:**

Pacing

- Spend 60-90 seconds per slide
- Allow for questions throughout
- Save 15 minutes for detailed Q&A
- Be prepared to skip or expand based on audience

Emphasis Points

- Lead with voice operations (most unique)
- Emphasize market opportunity (large TAM)
- Highlight traction if any exists
- Focus on buyer-specific value proposition

Customization by Audience

- Strategic buyers: Technology fit and customer synergy
- Financial buyers: Unit economics and scalability
- Corporate development: IP value and competitive positioning
- Private equity: Platform potential and value creation

Common Questions to Prepare For

- Why hasn't anyone else done this?
- What's the biggest risk?
- How long to first customer?
- What's the minimum acceptable exit?
- Who else are you talking to?
- What happens if this doesn't sell?
