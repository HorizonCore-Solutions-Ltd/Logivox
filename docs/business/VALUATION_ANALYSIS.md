## Value Detractors

### Critical Limitations

1. **No Customer Revenue: -40% to -60% of potential value**
   - No validated product-market fit
   - No recurring revenue metrics
   - No customer testimonials or case studies
   - No proven retention or satisfaction data

2. **Unproven Technology in Production**
   - Voice system requires real-world validation
   - No performance benchmarks from actual deployments
   - Integration challenges unknown
   - Scalability not demonstrated

3. **Market Risk**
   - No established brand recognition
   - No sales pipeline or qualified leads
   - No partner ecosystem
   - Unproven go-to-market strategy

4. **Competition Risk**
   - Well-established competitors (Manhattan, Blue Yonder, SAP)
   - High customer acquisition costs in enterprise software
   - Long sales cycles typical in industry

**Total Discount for Pre-Revenue Status:** 40-60%

## Recommended Minimum Acceptable Terms

### Financial Terms

**Minimum Cash at Closing:** $2,500,000

**Maximum Earnout Percentage:** 40% of total consideration

**Earnout Period:** Maximum 24 months

**Earnout Triggers:** Objective metrics only

- Revenue milestones
- Customer count thresholds
- Integration completion
- Never based on profitability (too easy to manipulate)

### Non-Financial Terms

**Intellectual Property:** Complete transfer with no retained rights

**Non-Compete:** Standard 2-3 year period, reasonable geographic scope

**Transition Period:** 3-6 months consulting availability

**Warranties:** Standard technology warranties, 12-18 month survival period

**Employment Requirements:** Flexible on key person retention
