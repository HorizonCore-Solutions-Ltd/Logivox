Subject to the timely payment of all subscription and usage fees, LogiVox grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to access and use the software for internal business operations and to manage 3PL client data where applicable.

* **API Fair Use**: While LogiVox supports extensive API integrations, usage must comply with our standard rate limits (detailed in API documentation). Malicious or reckless API flooding may result in temporary throttling.

## 2. Data Ownership & Security
* **Customer Data**: The Customer retains all intellectual property rights to the data uploaded into the system (inventory records, user data, financial metrics). 
* **Security Protocols**: LogiVox adheres to SOC 2 Type II compliance standards and enforces AES-256 encryption at rest. 
* **Data Portability**: Upon termination of the contract, the Customer may export their data in standard formats (CSV, JSON) for up to 30 days post-termination.
