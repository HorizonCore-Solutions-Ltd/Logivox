# LogiVox Master Service Level Agreement (SLA)

This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") outlines the operational guarantees, uptime commitments, and support response times provided by LogiVox Solutions, Inc. ("LogiVox") to its Enterprise and Professional tier customers ("Customer").

## 1. Uptime Commitment

LogiVox guarantees a Monthly Uptime Percentage of **99.99%** for the core Warehouse Management System (WMS) API and web application interfaces.

- **"Downtime"** is defined as consecutive periods of 5 minutes or more where the core API returns a 5XX error to valid requests, or the web dashboard is completely inaccessible.
- **"Exclusions"** include scheduled maintenance (notified at least 48 hours in advance) and force majeure events.

## 2. Platform Support Tiers & Response Times

### Standard Support (Growth Tier)

- **Coverage:** 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM EST, Monday – Friday.
- **Response Time (P1 Critical):** < 4 Hours
- **Response Time (General Question):** < 24 Hours

### Priority Support (Professional 3PL Tier)

- **Coverage:** 24x5 (Monday through Friday globally).
- **Response Time (P1 Critical):** < 1 Hour
- **Response Time (General/Bugs):** < 12 Hours

### Enterprise Support (Enterprise Command Tier)

- **Coverage:** 24/7/365
- **Response Time (P0 / System Down):** < 15 Minutes (Direct pager escalation to engineering)
- **Dedicated TAM:** Weekly check-ins and direct Slack/Teams channel access.

## 3. Service Credits

If LogiVox fails to meet the 99.99% Uptime Commitment in any given billing month, the Customer is eligible for Subscription Credits subject to the following calculation:

- Uptime < 99.9% but > 99.0%: 10% Credit
- Uptime < 99.0% but > 95.0%: 20% Credit
- Uptime < 95.0%: 30% Credit

To receive a credit, the Customer must submit a claim via the LogiVox Help Desk within 30 days of the incident.
