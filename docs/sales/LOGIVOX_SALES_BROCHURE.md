## 🚀 The Future of High-Volume Warehousing

LogiVox is a modern, AI-driven Warehouse Management System designed for 3PLs, E-Commerce fulfillment centers, and high-volume distribution networks. Unlike legacy systems that require months to deploy, LogiVox launches your operations in **60-90 days** with a fully cloud-native, API-first architecture.

### ✨ Core Product Offerings

**1. 3PL & Multi-Tenant Billing**
Operate multiple clients under the same roof with absolute data separation. Define individualized workflows, custom pick/pack profiles, and automate complex storage and transactional billing effortlessly.

**2. Intelligent Wave & Inventory Management**
Leverage dynamic wave routing to maximize order picking density. Utilize advanced inventory control including proactive cycle counting, optimized putaways, and dynamic bin sizing.

**3. Robotics & IoT Orchestration**
Connect directly with AMRs, AS/RS, sorters, and conveyor networks. LogiVox speaks directly to your automated hardware—no middleware required.

**4. Advanced Yard Management**
Gain full visibility over your trailers and dock doors. Optimize scheduling, reduce detention fees, and synchronize inbound flow directly to outbound put-walls.
