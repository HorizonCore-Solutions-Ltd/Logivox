# Enterprise Pricing Guide

## 1. Overview & Philosophy
This document serves as a comprehensive guide for the Sales and Marketing teams on how to structure, quote, and charge for Enterprise engagements. Enterprise pricing for Logivox is value-based, designed to align our pricing with the operational savings and scale we provide to large organizations.

Unlike our self-serve or standard tiers, Enterprise agreements are highly customized and consist of multiple cost components to handle complex requirements, massive scale, and dedicated support.

## 2. Core Pricing Components
Enterprise contracts should always be constructed using the following building blocks:

### A. Base Platform Licensing
*   **Model:** Tiered Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) based on system capacity, facility count, or total active users.
*   **Volume Discounts:** Scaling discounts apply as the client adds more facilities or user licenses.
*   **Minimum Commitment:** Enterprise tiers require a minimum 12-month contract, with 36-month terms preferred (incentivized with a 10-15% discount on the base license).

### B. Custom Implementation & Onboarding (Professional Services)
Enterprise deployments rarely work "out of the box." Charge a one-time setup fee based on the Statement of Work (SOW).
*   **Scoping:** Every enterprise deal requires a scoping phase.
*   **Implementation Fees:** Calculated by estimated consulting and engineering hours.
    *   *Standard Rate:* $200 - $250 / hour for Solutions Architects.
    *   *Data Migration:* Flat fee starting at $5,000 depending on legacy system complexity.
*   **Training:** On-site or remote dedicated training sessions are billed per day.

### C. API, Integrations, & Custom Requests
How we charge for special feature requests and integrations (ERP, WMS, CRM):
*   **Pre-built Connectors:** Monthly flat fee per active integration (e.g., standard SAP/Oracle connector).
*   **Custom Integrations:** Billed via Professional Services (see section B) for the initial build, plus an ongoing maintenance fee (usually 15-20% of the build cost annually) to ensure compatibility with future updates.
*   **Feature Requests:** If an enterprise requests a bespoke feature:
    *   *Exclusive Feature:* Client covers 100% of R&D and dev costs.
    *   *Roadmap Accelerated:* If it's already on our roadmap but they want it now, client covers 30-50% of the dev cost.

### D. Support & SLAs (Service Level Agreements)
Enterprise clients require robust support guarantees.
*   **Standard Enterprise Support:** Included in base license (24/5 email/chat support, 4-hour response time for critical issues).
*   **Premium Support (Upsell):** +20% of Base License. Includes 24/7 phone support, 1-hour critical response time, and a dedicated Technical Account Manager (TAM).

## 3. The Quoting Process & Modifying Requests
To ensure we do not underprice complex deployments, follow this process for every Enterprise request:

1.  **Discovery Call:** Identify scale, integration needs, and timeline. Do NOT provide quotes on this call.
2.  **Solutions Architecture Review:** Technical team reviews requirements to estimate Professional Services and Custom Integrations.
3.  **Quote Generation:** Structure the quote separating ARR (software, support) from NRR (Non-Recurring Revenue like setup, migration).
4.  **Approval:** Any discounts exceeding 15% on the Base Platform or waiving of Implementation fees must be approved by the VP of Sales or CRO.

## 4. Example Enterprise Proposal Structure
*   **Software License (ARR):** $120,000 / year (Includes up to 10 facilities, Unlimited standard users)
*   **ERP Integration (ARR):** $18,000 / year
*   **Premium Support (ARR):** $24,000 / year
*   **Implementation & Data Migration (One-time):** $45,000
*   **Total Year 1 Cost:** $207,000
    
*Review this guide quarterly to adjust consulting rates and baseline ARR as the product matures.*
