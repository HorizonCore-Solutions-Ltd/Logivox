# LogiVox Pricing Strategy & Market Positioning

*Confidential - Internal & Partner Use*

Our pricing strategy is designed specifically to disrupt legacy WMS providers (like Manhattan Associates, Oracle, and Korber). Legacy providers charge massive upfront licensing fees ("perpetual licenses") and require millions of dollars in implementation over 9 to 12 months. 

LogiVox offers a **transparent, predictable SaaS model** that makes it easy for our customers to understand exactly what they are buying, with absolutely zero hidden middleware fees.

## The Problem with Market Competitors
* **Legacy Competitors:** Charge $500k - $2M in year 1. They charge per named user and charge extra for API calls. Customers are confused and feel nickel-and-dimed.
* **Our Solution:** A flat, facility-and-feature based monthly subscription. We do not punish growth. We do not charge per user.

---

## LogiVox Transparent Pricing Tiers

We have simplified our pricing into three easy-to-understand packages.

### 1. The Growth Tier
**Target Audience:** D2C Brands outgrowing their Shopify/ShipStation setup, managing 1-3 warehouses.
* **Monthly Subscription:** $1,500 / month
* **One-Time Implementation:** $5,000 (covers onboarding, standard data migration, and training)
* **What They Get:**
  - Unlimited system users (no seat licenses).
  - Core WMS: Inventory tracking, wave picking, standard packing.
  - Standard integrations (Shopify, QuickBooks, standard shipping carriers).
* **The "Why":** Gets them off spreadsheets immediately without a terrifying capital expenditure.

### 2. The Professional 3PL Tier
**Target Audience:** Multi-client fulfillment centers (3PLs) who need to bill their own customers.
* **Monthly Subscription:** $4,500 / month
* **One-Time Implementation:** $15,000 (inclusive of custom billing rules setup)
* **What They Get:**
  - Everything in Growth, plus:
  - **3PL Multi-Tenant Module:** Absolute data separation for their clients.
  - **Automated Billing Engine:** Automatically generate storage and pick/pack invoices.
  - **Client Portals:** Their customers can log in and see their own inventory.
* **The "Why":** A 3PL's biggest nightmare is billing their clients manually. This tier pays for itself by capturing leaked revenue.

### 3. The Enterprise Command Tier
**Target Audience:** Massive distribution networks, robotics-heavy sites, and global retailers.
* **Monthly Subscription:** Custom (Starting at $10,000 / month)
* **One-Time Implementation:** Custom (Starting at $40,000)
* **What They Get:**
  - **IoT & Robotics Orchestration Core:** Direct deep integration into automated hardware.
  - **Advanced Yard Management (YMS):** Control trailers and dock doors.
  - **Custom Edge Network & Dedicated DB:** Highest performance possible.
  - **Dedicated Technical Account Manager.**
* **The "Why":** Enterprise needs high predictability and 99.99% uptime. They are fleeing Oracle because Oracle cannot integrate with modern robotics easily. LogiVox is plug-and-play.

## Discount & Contract Mechanics
* **Annual Contracts:** Offer a 15% discount on the monthly SaaS fee if paid entirely upfront annually.
* **Volume Scaling:** If an Enterprise signs a 5-year agreement across 10+ facilities, implementation costs can be amortized over the first 12 months to remove capital friction.
