# LogiVox Sales Playbook

This playbook provides foundational messaging, scripts, and objection-handling techniques to sell LogiVox effectively. 

Our core philosophy: **"Sell the solution to their headache, not just the software."**

## 1. Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs)

### A. The Scaling D2C Brand (Growth Tier)
* **Pain Point:** Operating out of spreadsheets or Shopify directly. Mis-ships are high, inventory is constantly inaccurate, and fulfillment is too slow.
* **Our Pitch:** "LogiVox organizes your floor. We implement barcode-driven wave picking and live inventory counts. We stop your warehouse from being a liability and turn it into a growth engine."

### B. The Frustrated 3PL (Professional Tier)
* **Pain Point:** Manually calculating billing at the end of the month via Excel. Their clients are demanding portal access that they cannot provide.
* **Our Pitch:** "LogiVox automates your bottom line. Our 3PL module specifically tracks all client-specific activity—storage, inbound pallets, outbound picks—and generates the invoice automatically. Plus, we give your clients a secure portal to see their stock without calling you."

### C. The Enterprise Network (Enterprise Tier)
* **Pain Point:** Locked into a 15-year-old on-premise system. Looking to add robotics (Locus/AMRs) but their current WMS can't connect without a $500k middleware project.
* **Our Pitch:** "LogiVox is API-first. We plug directly into your AMRs and conveyors natively. We'll deploy faster than legacy systems and drastically reduce your total cost of ownership."

---

## 2. Common Objections & How to Handle Them

### Objection: "Your system is too new. Oracle gives us security."
**Response:** "Oracle gives you 1990s architecture. LogiVox is built on the same cloud-native infrastructure that powers Netflix and Shopify. We are SOC 2 compliant, fully encrypted, and our deployment phase is 60 days compared to Oracle's 12 months. Let’s do a low-risk pilot in one facility."

### Objection: "We already have an ERP (NetSuite), do we need a WMS?"
**Response:** "NetSuite is fantastic for financials, but it fails on the warehouse floor. It doesn't do intelligent pick-path routing or dynamic wave allocation. LogiVox sits beneath NetSuite, orchestrating the physical labor while instantly updating NetSuite's financials via our native API connector."

### Objection: "The $15,000 implementation fee is too high."
**Response:** "This isn’t just software access. Our engineers actively build your facility maps, set up your billing rules, test your barcode scanners, and train your staff. We guarantee a successful launch. If you get it wrong doing it yourself with a cheaper vendor, the operational downtime will cost 10x our implementation fee."

---

## 3. The Demo Flow
1. **Discovery (15 mins):** Ask 80% of the questions. Understand their pain points. Are they a 3PL? Do they use NetSuite? 
2. **The "Aha" Moment (10 mins):** Show them exactly the screen that solves their pain. (e.g., Show the automated invoice generation for 3PLs).
3. **The Proof (10 mins):** Show the mobile scanner interface. Keep it simple. 
4. **Next Steps (5 mins):** Book the scoping call with a Solutions Engineer. Do not talk pricing until Discovery is complete.
