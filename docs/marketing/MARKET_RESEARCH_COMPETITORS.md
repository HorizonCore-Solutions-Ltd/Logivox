# 📊 Market Research: The "Hybrid Choice" Pivot

> **Date:** March 4, 2026
> **Subject:** Competitor Weakness Analysis & Strategic Pivot to "Adaptive UI"

## 1. Executive Summary

Our research into the "Big Boys" of the warehouse voice market (Honeywell Vocollect, Zebra, Lucas Systems) reveals a polarized landscape that neglects a critical user need: **Flexibility**.

- **Honeywell (Vocollect):** Forces "Screenless Only". Proprietary hardware (~$2k/unit). locked-in ecosystem.
- **Zebra:** Forces "Screen Heavy". Voice is just an app on a touch computer. Eyes are often glued to the screen, defeating the purpose of voice.
- **The Gap:** Customers want the _speed_ of screenless for experts, but the _ease_ of screens for new hires. They hate buying $150 batteries for proprietary devices.

## 2. Competitor Analysis

### 🛑 Honeywell Vocollect

- **Philosophy:** "Eyes-Free, Hands-Free" (Dogmatic).
- **Hardware:** Talkman A700 series. No screen.
- **Pain Points:**
  - **Training Hell:** New hires are "blind". They have to memorize audio trees. Training takes 2-3 weeks to reach proficiency.
  - **Exception handling:** Dealing with a damaged barcode or short pick requires complex voice menus ("Say 'Special Functions', Say 'Short'").
  - **Cost:** Extremely high TCO (Total Cost of Ownership).

### 🛑 Zebra / Lucas Systems

- **Philosophy:** "Multi-modal" (but screen-first).
- **Hardware:** TC5x Android Touch Computers.
- **Pain Points:**
  - **Distraction:** Users constantly look at the screen to confirm validation, slowing them down.
  - **Battery Drain:** screens on 100% brightness kill batteries in 4 hours.
  - **Ergonomics:** Holding a heavy brick all day causes fatigue.

## 3. The "VPick.ai" Advantage: Adaptive UI

We are pivoting VPick.ai (LogiVox Voice) to be the **only** platform that offers "Hybrid Choice" on standard hardware.

### The 3 Modes of Operation

1.  **🎓 Rookie Mode (Visual + Voice)**
    - _Persona:_ New Hire (Day 1 - Day 14).
    - _UI behavior:_ Screen ON. Shows product images, maps, and large text instructions.
    - _Goal:_ Accuracy & Training. "Visual confirmation reduces anxiety."

2.  **⚡ Pro Mode (Voice Dominant)**
    - _Persona:_ Experienced Picker (Month 1+).
    - _UI behavior:_ Screen DIMMED (OLED Black). Wakes only for exceptions (Short picks, errors) or when user asks "Show me".
    - _Goal:_ Battery Life & Speed.

3.  **🚀 Speed Mode (Pure Voice)**
    - _Persona:_ The "10-Year Veteran".
    - _UI behavior:_ Screen OFF / Pocketed. 100% Audio interaction.
    - _Goal:_ Maximum throughput. Hands completely free.
